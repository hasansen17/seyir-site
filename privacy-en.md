# Seyir — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026 · [Türkçe](/gizlilik)

Seyir is an app and website for following Turkish TV series. This page explains, briefly and clearly, what data Seyir collects, why, and what happens to it. It is also the information notice under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). If this English version and the [Turkish version](/gizlilik) differ, the Turkish version prevails.

**Data controller:** Hasan Şen (individual developer) — seyirapp.help@gmail.com

## In short

- You don't need an account to browse series, search or check the TV schedule; no data about you is collected while you do.
- For personal features such as your watchlist, ratings, favorites, lists, comments and notifications, you sign in **with Apple**, **with Google** or **with email + password**. No password is stored for Apple and Google sign-in; if you sign up with email, your password is stored one-way encrypted (irreversibly) on the server.
- Once you sign in, your email address and the data you create in the app (watch statuses, ratings, favorites, lists, comments, who you follow) are stored on our server, linked to your account, so they aren't lost if you change phones.
- Seyir is a community: **your username, watch statuses, ratings, favorites, lists, comments and follower/following lists are visible to other users.** There are no private profiles. **Your email address is never shown to anyone.**
- No ads, no usage analytics, no third-party tracking tools. Your data is not sold or shared with anyone.
- You can delete your account at any time in Settings; everything on the server is deleted.

## Account and the data collected

When you sign in, the following is stored:

- **Sign-in identifier:** a random code generated by Apple or Google for your account (with Apple sign-in it is unique to Seyir). With email sign-up, your email address and a one-way encrypted version of your password are stored instead; your password cannot be read, not even by us.
- **Your email address:** comes from Apple, Google or you. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", Apple's relay address is stored instead of your real address, which is enough for us. Your email is used only to identify your account and for account actions (sign-up verification, password reset); no newsletters or ads are sent. When you sign up with email or reset your password, a 6-digit verification code is sent to your address.
- **Your username:** you choose it on the "Welcome" screen right after signing in; it is public and can be changed once every 30 days. Your real name is not requested: Apple sign-in only asks for your email; with Google sign-in, the name and profile picture Google sends are **not stored** — only your email and Google's user code are kept.
- **Your profile photo (optional):** if you choose one from your gallery or camera, a reduced copy (256×256) is stored on the server and shown to everyone on your profile. The photo sits at a public web address; anyone who knows the address can see it outside the app, so don't choose a photo you don't want to share. When you tap "Remove photo", the file is deleted. **Cover image:** if you pick a TMDB scene photo from a series, only your choice is stored; **if you upload a photo from your gallery**, the cropped area is reduced (about 1500×600), stored on the server and shown to everyone on your profile. Like the profile photo, the cover photo sits at a public address; "Remove cover" deletes the file.
- **Data you create in the app:** your watch statuses (Watchlist, Watching, watched marks), ratings you give to series, episode ratings and reactions, quiz results (score and time; shown with your username in a series' top 10) and quiz requests, favorite series and actors, your lists and their contents, your comments and replies, your likes, who you follow and your followers, users you block, and reports you make.
- **In-app notification list:** replies, likes and new followers you receive are kept on the server for 30 days to be shown on the Notifications page, then deleted.
- **Usage record:** for signed-in accounts, when you last opened the app ("last seen"), on which days you used it (by day, no time of day), and which platform you used (iPhone, Android or web) are stored. This is used only for aggregate numbers (e.g. "how many people opened the app today"); when someone signed in is not shown to other users, not reviewed individually, and not used for ads or profiling. Daily usage rows are deleted after 400 days; everything is deleted when you delete your account.

This data is stored on Seyir's server (Supabase infrastructure, Frankfurt, Germany). Your profile data (watch statuses, ratings, favorites, lists, comments, follow lists) is visible to other users; users you block and users who block you can't see it. Your blocks and reports are visible only to you and the Seyir administrator. Your series ratings also count toward the series' average "Seyir rating". **Your episode ratings and reactions are only shown as totals** (the episode's average rating, how many people gave which reaction); who gave what is never shown to anyone and does not appear on your profile.

If you collected lists and ratings on your phone before creating an account, they are moved to your account the first time you sign in.

## Comments, reports and moderation

Comments on series and actor pages are public; people without an account can read them too. Comments pass through an automatic inappropriate-word filter before they are posted. Reported content and users are reviewed by the Seyir administrator (the app's developer); decisions (removal, suspension, ban) are recorded with the date and reason. A banned account's content is deleted; to prevent the same person from creating a new account, only the ban record with an irreversible digest of the sign-in identifier (Apple/Google code) or email address is kept. Comments reported by more than one user are hidden automatically until the review is complete.

## Stored on your phone

- To open faster, a copy (cache) of series and actor information and of your account data is kept on your phone. When you sign out, the personal copy is cleared.
- On the website, your language choice (Turkish/English) is remembered in your browser.

## Photo and camera permission

If you want to choose a profile or cover photo, the app asks for access to your gallery or camera. Only the photo you choose is cropped, reduced and uploaded; the rest of your gallery is not accessed. If you don't grant permission, the app keeps working without photos. Inappropriate profile and cover photos can be reported and are removed by the administrator.

## Notifications

If you turn on notifications (the app asks; if you decline, nothing is sent), your device's notification address (a random code issued by Apple and Expo to deliver notifications, a "push token") and your phone's language (Turkish or English, so notifications arrive in your language) are stored on the server, linked to your account. Each notification type can be turned off separately in Settings: new episode (every evening, if any series you follow has a new episode that day), replies to your comment, likes on your comment, new follower, quiz (when the quiz for a series you requested opens). Notifications are not used for anything else (ads, announcements). When you turn all notifications off or sign out, the record is deleted.

## Data fetched from the internet

The series list and series and actor details are downloaded from Seyir's own server. Posters and actor photos load directly from TMDB's servers. As with any internet connection, these servers may see your device's IP address and the time of the request in technical logs; Seyir does not use this to identify you.

## Service providers

Your data is processed only by the infrastructure providers needed to run the app:
- **Supabase** (US company; servers in Frankfurt, Germany): database, file storage (profile and cover photos) and authentication.
- **Apple** (US): Sign in with Apple and delivery of notifications to your phone.
- **Google** (US): Sign in with Google; sign-up verification and password reset emails are also sent via Gmail (seyirapp.help@gmail.com), so your email address passes through Google's mail servers.
- **Expo** (US): delivery of notifications and distribution of app updates (EAS Update); it may see your device's IP address during update checks, which we don't receive.
- **GitHub** (US): hosts the seyirdizi.app website.

No data is transferred to any other organization; it may be shared with the competent authorities only if required by law.

**Transfers abroad:** your data is processed on servers in Germany (Supabase) and, for authentication, email delivery, notifications, updates and website hosting, in the US (Apple, Google, Expo, GitHub). This transfer is based on the explicit consent you give on the "Welcome" screen; you can withdraw it by deleting your account.

## Data sources

Series, actor and image information is provided by [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. "Where to watch" information comes from JustWatch. Seyir's news stories are written by Seyir from public sources listed under each story; English versions of stories, quizzes and some series summaries are translated automatically.

## Children

Seyir is for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we find that a child has created an account, we delete it; you can write to us.

## Purposes and legal grounds (KVKK Art. 5)

- Creating an account, authentication, storing your lists and ratings, providing comments and following: **establishing and performing the contract** (required for you to use the app).
- Moderation of inappropriate content, reviewing reports, ban records, security: **legitimate interest** and **legal obligation** (protecting the community and other users).
- Profile and cover photos and optional notifications: **your explicit consent** (you can remove/turn them off at any time).
- Transfers abroad: **your explicit consent** (above).

Data is processed only for these purposes and for as long as your account is open; when you delete your account, everything except the ban record is deleted.

## Your rights (KVKK Art. 11): access, correction, deletion

- All data in your account is visible in the app; username, lists, ratings, comments and favorites can be changed in the app.
- **Delete my account:** Settings → Account → "Delete my account". Your account, email address, username, profile and cover photos and all your data (including comments, likes and follows) are permanently deleted from the server; if you signed in with Apple, the Apple connection is revoked, and the copy on your phone is cleared. Comments that received replies remain as "[deleted]" without author information. This cannot be undone.
- **Sign out:** your account stays, and the personal copy on your phone is cleared.
- Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information, learn whether it is used for its purpose, know the parties it is transferred to, request correction, deletion or destruction, request that these actions be notified to those parties, object to a result against you arising from automated analysis, and claim compensation if you suffer damage.
- **Requests:** if you can't do these in the app or want to exercise your rights, write to **seyirapp.help@gmail.com** with your account's username. Requests are resolved free of charge within **30 days** at the latest. If you are not satisfied with the result, you may complain to Turkey's Personal Data Protection Board.

## Security

Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS); on the server, rules ensure each user can change only their own data and only they can see their non-public data (email, blocks, reports). No password is stored for Apple and Google sign-in; with email sign-up, the password is stored only one-way encrypted (irreversibly).

## Future changes

If new features are added, this page is updated and the change is announced in the app. Before any new data starts being collected, this page will clearly state what is collected.
