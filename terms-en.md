# Seyir — Terms of Use and Community Guidelines

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026 · [Türkçe](/kosullar)

By using Seyir and accepting on the "Welcome" screen after signing in, you agree to the terms below. We kept them short; please read them. If this English version and the [Turkish version](/kosullar) differ, the Turkish version prevails.

## 1. What Seyir is
Seyir is a free app and website for following Turkish TV series. Series information comes from TMDB; Seyir does not guarantee its accuracy. Some content (English news, quizzes and summaries) is translated automatically. The service is provided "as is", with no promise of uninterrupted operation.

## 2. Account
- An account requires signing in with Apple, Google or email + password. You are responsible for everything done from your account; don't share your password with anyone.
- Seyir is for **ages 13 and up**. If you are under 13, don't create an account.
- Usernames are 3–20 characters of lowercase letters, digits and underscores; names that impersonate someone, are insulting or misleading will be changed.
- One person, one account. You may not transfer your account to someone else.

## 3. Community guidelines — zero tolerance
The following are **prohibited** in comments, usernames, profile photos and lists:
- Insults, threats, harassment, bullying; attacks targeting a person.
- Hate speech based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar grounds.
- Obscene, pornographic or violence-glorifying content; inappropriate profile photos (obscenity, hate symbols, someone else's photo).
- Spam, advertising, chain messages, link dumps.
- Sharing other people's personal information.
- Impersonating someone else or Seyir.
- Illegal content and copyright infringement.
- About actors and other real people: claims about their private lives, defamation, insults about their body or appearance. Criticism of acting and roles is fine; personal attacks are not.

Also: post comments containing spoilers **with the "Spoiler" mark**. Unmarked spoilers can be reported and hidden.

Content that breaks these rules is **removed without warning**; the account may be suspended (7 days) or permanently closed. Zero tolerance applies to inappropriate content and harassment.

## 4. Reports, blocking and moderation
- Every comment and profile has **"Report"**. Reports are reviewed within **24 hours** at the latest; comments reported by more than one user are hidden automatically until the review is complete.
- You can **block** any user; someone you block can't follow you or see your comments and profile, and you won't see theirs.
- The Seyir administrator may remove any content and suspend or close any account. To appeal, write to seyirapp.help@gmail.com.
- The app uses an automatic inappropriate-word filter; comments caught by the filter are not posted.
